Kitsune Vi

Hello, my name is Violet (born 2003), I am a mathematician and functional programmer. I have been self studying pure mathematics since I was 11 and picked up programming by the age of 16. Nothing satisfies me more then a well crafted abstraction that not only solves the problem, but describes it so naturally that no desire is left save for a truly radical reimagining.

I dream of an ecosystem that treats software as an art in this way, where problems are deliberated over until they can truly be called solved.

I hope you find what you are looking for on my site!
